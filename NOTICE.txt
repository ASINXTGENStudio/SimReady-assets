SimReady pack 1.0.0
Contains 3D models converted from NVIDIA SimReady assets (https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/simready-assets),
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). (c) NVIDIA Corporation.
Changes: meshes decimated, textures downsized to JPEG albedo only, collision shapes recomputed (convex hulls / CoACD
decomposition), masses checked (some replaced by typical real-world masses), small joints of hand-held things frozen,
converted to MuJoCo (.msh) and glTF binary (.glb). No endorsement by NVIDIA is implied.
Each asset's original name is kept as its id; the list is in manifest.json.
